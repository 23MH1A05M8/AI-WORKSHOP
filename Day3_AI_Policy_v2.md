# Placement Cell AI-Use Policy
**Name:** HADASSA KUNISETTY | **Effective Date:** 10-06-2026 

---

## 1. Risk Classification of Student-AI Scenarios (EU AI Act Tiers)

| # | Scenario | Tier | Reasoning |
|---|----------|------|-----------|
| 1 | AI used to research a company before an interview (news, products, culture) | **Minimal** | Preparation tool with no third-party impact. Equivalent to reading the company website. Free use. |
| 2 | AI-generated cover letter submitted as the student's own | **Limited** | Cover letters vary in how much "student voice" recruiters expect. Allowed with declaration; student must be able to defend every claim made in it. |
| 3 | AI used to translate or simplify a job description the student does not fully understand | **Minimal** | Comprehension aid. No representation is made to any recruiter. Free use. |
| 4 | AI used to fill in online aptitude/reasoning test sections during a live proctored assessment | **Unacceptable** | Direct academic dishonesty in a recruiter-administered evaluation. Banned outright. Equivalent to cheating in an exam. |
| 5 | AI-generated LinkedIn summary and headline presented as self-authored | **Limited** | Public professional profile viewed by recruiters. Allowed with awareness that the student must verbally back every claim; no fabrication of skills or experience. |
| 6 | AI used to produce a fake offer letter or internship certificate | **Unacceptable** | Document forgery. Banned outright regardless of circumstance. |
| 7 | AI chatbot used to answer HR questions live during a video interview (earpiece or screen assist) | **Unacceptable** | Real-time deception of a recruiter conducting an evaluation. Identity and competence fraud. Banned outright. |
| 8 | AI used to summarise feedback from past mock interviews and identify recurring weak areas | **Minimal** | Self-improvement tool. No claim is made to any external party. Free use. |

## 2. Permitted Uses

Students **may** use AI for the following, subject to stated conditions:

1. **Research companies before interviews** — news, recent product launches, leadership, culture — free use with no declaration required.
2. **Translate or simplify complex job descriptions** to better understand role requirements — free use.
3. **Draft a cover letter** — allowed when the student reviews it fully, removes any inaccurate claims, and can speak to every sentence if questioned.
4. **Build a LinkedIn profile summary** — allowed when all skills, tools, and experience listed are things the student has genuinely worked with.
5. **Analyse personal mock-interview feedback** to identify patterns and prioritise preparation areas — free use.
6. **Generate a list of likely interview questions** for a specific role or company, then practice answering them independently — free use.

---

## 3. Prohibited Uses

Students **may not** use AI for the following. Each rule includes a detection method.

1. **Use AI assistance during a live proctored aptitude, reasoning, or coding assessment** administered by a recruiter.
   *Detection:* Placement cell cross-checks anomalous score jumps between internal assessments and recruiter-administered results; flags cases where a student cannot solve a simpler version of the same problem type in a follow-up placement cell session.

2. **Submit a fake offer letter, internship certificate, or any AI-generated credential document** as evidence of prior experience.
   *Detection:* Placement cell contacts the issuing organisation directly to verify all experience documents before including the student in shortlists.

3. **Use an AI tool (via earpiece, phone, or secondary screen) to answer questions in real time during a live recruiter interview or group discussion.**
   *Detection:* Placement cell debrief after each drive — students who clear rounds are asked to walk through 2–3 answers they gave; inconsistencies between the debrief and the reported interview performance are escalated.

4. **Present an AI-generated LinkedIn profile that lists skills, tools, or certifications the student has not actually used or earned.**
   *Detection:* Mentor conducts a 10-minute profile verification conversation before approving the student's profile for recruiter-facing sharing; any listed skill is fair game for a spot question.

---

## 4. Enforcement

The placement cell will conduct post-placement verification for 25% of placed students each cycle, including a document check and a short debrief conversation on the student's reported experience and assessment performance. Violations of Prohibited Uses result in: **first offence** — written warning, mandatory re-verification of all submitted documents, and a 30-day suspension from placement activities; **second offence** — permanent withdrawal of placement cell support and a report to the academic integrity committee. The placement cell acknowledges it cannot monitor AI use during off-campus recruiter assessments; this policy therefore relies in part on student integrity and on each student's understanding that misrepresentation discovered after joining a company leads to termination and permanent reputational damage.
